01 — About

The short version

I build production software in fintech — React Native apps on Android and iOS, and the Spring Boot services behind them. Two-plus years at ZET, most of it spent on the unglamorous half: API design, state that stays correct, and knowing what broke after a release.

I came into engineering through Masai School after an electrical engineering degree, which is a long way of saying I learned this by building things rather than by being told how. That has not really changed.
