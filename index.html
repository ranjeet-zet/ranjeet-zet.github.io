OTA & Release Platform

Built at ZET · in production · 2026

A self-hosted over-the-air update and mobile release platform for the ZET React Native app — designed and built end to end, now the path JS releases ship through. A JS-only fix reaches users without waiting on a store review.

  • Per-device update targeting with staged rollout and one-step rollback, so a release can be widened or pulled without shipping a new build.
  • Native builds and store releases driven from a single console on top of GitHub Actions, replacing a manual build-and-upload routine.
  • Remote config, crash reporting and release alerting, with a Next.js dashboard over the whole pipeline.
  • Went from proposal to the team’s production release path, built alongside regular feature work.

Amrutkaal

Founder & Developer · 2026 — Present

A free, ad-free daily Hindu Panchang and Vedic calendar built from astronomical first principles — not copied from a reference city.

  • Panchang for 546,462 Indian cities and 162,646 cities across 84 countries, each computed individually from local sunrise, sunset and timezone.
  • Swiss Ephemeris on NASA JPL DE431 with Lahiri ayanamsa; Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga and Karana derived from sidereal longitudes by bisection search, accurate to ±1 minute — sunrise/sunset to ±30 seconds, validated against Drik Panchang.
  • Free Janma Kundali (Lagna + 12 houses, 9 grahas, North/South Indian and Navamsa charts), 120-year Vimshottari Dasha, Ashtakavarga, and 36-point Gun Milan matching.
  • Daily Muhurat output — Rahu Kaal, Choghadiya, Abhijit Muhurat and auspicious windows — plus an audio Panchang.
  • Side tools: Rashi calculator, daily Rashifal, Tithi calculator, baby names by Nakshatra, a multi-faith festival calendar through 2030, eclipse data and prayer times.
  • Worldwide and multilingual — dedicated Panchang for 10 countries, English and Hindi UI, and typo-tolerant city search.
  • All chart math runs in the browser — name, date, time and place are never transmitted or stored.
